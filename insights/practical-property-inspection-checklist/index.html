A Practical Property Inspection Checklist for Homes in Accra

Published by Civitas Editorial • November 2026 • 5 min read

Whether you are evaluating a newly completed building in East Legon, monitoring an investment home in Community 25, or assessing your own residence before the rains, a methodical walkthrough checklist reveals issues before they escalate.

Room-by-Room Inspection Guide

1. Exterior & Boundary Walls

  • Inspect compound walls for structural settlement cracks or leaning.
  • Check razor wire, spikes, or electric fence tension and test energizer alarm functions.
  • Ensure compound stormwater drainage channels slope toward municipal gutters without pooling.

2. Roof, Gutters & Ceiling Plaster

  • Look for yellowish water rings or brownish moisture stains on POP or gypsum ceilings.
  • Inspect valley gutters and downspouts for leaves, dead vegetation, or bird nesting.
  • Verify roof flashing sealant around vents, parapets, and rooftop solar mounting frames.

3. Plumbing & Water Systems

  • Check water meter movement when all taps and appliances are shut off.
  • Inspect beneath bathroom vanities for slow drips from flexible hoses and angle valves.
  • Flush toilets and observe whether the fill valve shuts off cleanly or trickles continuously.

4. Electrical Distribution Board

  • Feel breaker switches for unusual warmth or buzzing sounds.
  • Test Residual Current Devices (RCD / safety trip switches) using the built-in test button.
  • Check that all switchboards and plug sockets are securely anchored to the wall.
