Why Preventive Maintenance Matters

Published by Civitas Editorial • November 2026 • 4 min read

"If it isn't broken, don't fix it" is a costly philosophy in real estate. Buildings deteriorate quietly: microscopic seal failures lead to soaked plaster, unserviced AC units burn out compressors, and unlubricated security locks fail when you need them most.

Reactive vs. Preventive Property Management

Reactive maintenance waits for an active crisis—a burst pipe at midnight, an electrical fire smell in the consumer unit, or water pouring through a gypsum ceiling during a storm. Emergency repairs almost always involve premium contractor rates, structural collateral damage, and severe occupant inconvenience.

Preventive maintenance is scheduled, deliberate, and budgeted. Replacing a GH₵25 washer ring during a routine check prevents GH₵4,000 in ruined laminate flooring and soaked cabinetry.

Three Core Benefits of Planned Care

  • Capital Preservation: Well-maintained properties retain significantly higher rental yields and resale appraisal values across Accra and Tema.
  • Predictable Expenditures: A monthly care plan replaces erratic, multi-thousand-cedi emergency surprises with calm, planned maintenance allowances.
  • Longer Equipment Lifespan: Regular cleaning and lubrication extend the operational lifespan of water pumps, generator sets, and inverter air conditioning units by years.
