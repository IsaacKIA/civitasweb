How Often Should You Deep Clean Your Home?

Published by Civitas Editorial • November 2026 • 4 min read

There is a distinct difference between everyday routine cleaning and the intensive sanitation known as deep cleaning. In Greater Accra and Tema, environmental factors like harmattan dust and coastal humidity make this distinction vital for maintaining a healthy living space.

Routine Cleaning vs. Deep Cleaning

Routine cleaning covers the surface maintenance tasks that occur weekly or bi-weekly: sweeping, mopping high-traffic tile floors, wiping visible kitchen counters, and cleaning toilets and washbasins. It keeps your home presentable and prevents obvious dust build-up.

Deep cleaning goes significantly deeper. It focuses on the areas that routine maintenance misses: scrubbing tile grout lines where soap scum and mildew accumulate, washing behind large kitchen appliances, dusting ceiling fans and high light fixtures, deep-sanitizing oven interiors, and wiping door frames, baseboards, and window tracks.

The Recommended Frequency for Ghanaian Homes

For most households in Accra and Tema, we recommend scheduling an intensive deep cleaning every 3 to 4 months (or once every quarter). However, specific events warrant an immediate deep clean:

  • Post-Harmattan (February–March): After months of dry northeastern winds depositing fine red dust across window tracks, louvers, and upholstery.
  • Before Rainy Season: Sanitizing moisture-prone areas to prevent early mildew growth on tiles and ceilings.
  • Before or After Major Family Gatherings: Restoring the home to immaculate condition after heavy occupancy.
  • Move-In / Move-Out Turnovers: Eliminating lingering odors, allergens, and grime before settling into a new property.

Key Benefits of Scheduled Deep Cleaning

Beyond aesthetics, periodic deep cleaning protects your property's value. Accumulated grime etches into sanitary ceramics, mineral deposits ruin chrome taps, and uncleaned AC filters force compressors to overwork, driving up electricity tariffs.
