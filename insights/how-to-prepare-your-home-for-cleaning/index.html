How to Prepare Your Home for Professional Cleaning

Published by Civitas Editorial • November 2026 • 3 min read

Hiring a professional cleaning service allows you to reclaim your weekend. Taking five minutes to prepare your space beforehand helps the cleaning personnel focus their energy where it counts most: intensive scrubbing and sanitization.

1. Clear Countertop Clutter

Put away loose paperwork, clothing, and children's toys from tables and kitchen worktops. When surfaces are clear, cleaners can immediately begin dusting, wiping, and sanitizing without spending half their booking organizing personal items.

2. Secure Important Documents & Valuables

While professional cleaning personnel are vetted for integrity, it is best practice to secure passports, jewelry, cash, and sensitive legal papers in a locked safe or private drawer to prevent accidental misplacement.

3. Point Out Fragile or Delicate Finishes

If you have unsealed natural stone, custom antique mirrors, or specialty hand-painted cabinetry, notify the team leader during the initial walkthrough so appropriate non-abrasive cleaners are used.

4. Ensure Access to Running Water & Electricity

Vacuum extraction machines, floor scrubbers, and pressure washers require dependable electrical voltage and water flow. Check that poly-tanks have water and pre-paid meters have sufficient units before arrival.
