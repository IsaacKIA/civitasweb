Managing Your Property from Abroad: What to Monitor

Published by Civitas Editorial • November 2026 • 6 min read

Building or buying property in Ghana while living in the UK, North America, or Europe is a proud milestone. But remote oversight presents unique challenges: reliance on busy relatives, irregular reports, and discovering severe maintenance damage only when you return home for the holidays.

The Five Critical Risk Points to Monitor Remotely

1. Moisture Ingress & Coastal Corrosion

Tropical rainstorms and saline coastal breezes (especially across Tema, Sakumono, and coastal Accra) accelerate metal corrosion and roof leak penetration. Without periodic inspections, moisture can seep into electrical conduits and destroy internal finishes undetected.

2. Perimeter Security & Boundary Integrity

Regular visual checks of compound walls, access gates, razor wire/electric fencing, and gate locks deter unauthorized entry and confirm whether compound vegetation requires trimming.

3. Water Storage & Stagnation

Overhead water tanks and booster pumps in unoccupied properties should not sit stagnant for months. Regular checks verify that ball valves are tight, lids are secured against insects, and pumps do not seize from disuse.

4. Utility Consumption & Unpaid Tariffs

An unexpected surge in water bills or pre-paid electricity meters often reveals an unaddressed plumbing leak or unauthorized tapping into the compound supply.

5. Independent Documentation Without Family Strain

Asking family members or friends to repeatedly inspect, photograph, and manage artisans often strains relationships. A professional service delivers unbiased, dated photographic evidence directly to your WhatsApp or inbox without awkwardness.
