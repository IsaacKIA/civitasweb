Pre-Rainy Season Roof & Drainage Maintenance: Protect Your Accra Home

Published by Civitas Structural Maintenance • October 2026 • 6 min read

In Greater Accra and Tema, tropical downpours test every weld, screw, seal, and gutter on your property. When the rains arrive in April and May, emergency roof repairs become exponentially harder to execute — and water damage to POP ceilings, electrical circuits, and parquet flooring can cost tens of thousands of Ghana Cedis.

Why Fixing Leaks During the Rains is a Costly Mistake

Many property owners wait until water is dripping onto their living room rug before calling an artisan. But waterproofing membranes, silicone sealants, and bitumen flashings require dry surfaces to adhere properly. Trying to patch a leaking roof in the middle of a thunderstorm is ineffective, dangerous, and at best a temporary band-aid.

The Pre-Rain 5-Point Walkthrough Checklist

1. Parapet Wall & Roof Flashing Resealing

Where corrugated sheets or concrete roof slabs meet masonry parapet walls is the primary entry point for moisture ingress. Re-applying elastomeric waterproof membranes before March ensures persistent rains cannot seep behind your walls.

2. Gutter & Downspout De-clogging

Throughout the dry months, dead leaves, harmattan silt, and bird droppings choke PVC gutters. When the first heavy storm hits, backed-up water overflows behind fascia boards, rotting timber and soaking plaster ceilings.

3. Soakaway Pit & Compound Grating Flow Test

Pouring test buckets through courtyard floor drains and inspection chambers reveals slow drainage and blockages before stormwater ponds in your driveway or threatens to breach your doorstep.

4. Electrical Earthing & Surge Protection Check

Tropical storms bring severe lightning surges. A certified electrician must verify earth electrode resistance and install functional surge diverters in your distribution board to protect appliances and inverter installations.

5. Sump Pump & Submersible Pump Test

For homes with basements, low-lying compounds, or underground water pits, testing that automatic float switches engage and pump water rapidly is vital before the first flood advisory.

Pre-Rainy Season FAQs for Accra Property Owners

When should I prepare my roof and drainage in Accra?

Between January and March. Sealants and waterproof membranes require completely dry surfaces to cure and bond effectively.

How much does gutter cleaning cost in Accra?

Gutter de-clogging and downspout flushing typically costs GH₵ 150 to 250 for residential homes in Accra & Tema.
