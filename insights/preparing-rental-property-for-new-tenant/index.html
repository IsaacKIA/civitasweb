Preparing a Rental Property for a New Tenant

Published by Civitas Editorial • November 2026 • 5 min read

The condition in which a tenant receives a rental property sets the standard for how they will care for it. A clean, properly maintained handover eliminates initial disputes, protects security deposits, and attracts responsible, long-term tenants.

Key Handover Preparation Steps

1. Professional Move-In Deep Clean

Standard sweeping is insufficient between tenancies. Ensure sanitary fixtures are de-scaled, kitchen grease is cleared, window tracks are dusted, and tile grout is scrubbed clean.

2. Plumbing Audit & Pressure Testing

Check every faucet, showerhead, and toilet cistern. Replace worn washer rings, ensure angle valves turn smoothly, and verify that hot water heaters and booster pumps function safely.

3. Lock & Key Verification

For tenant security, replace primary entrance lock cylinders between occupants. Ensure all interior keys (bedrooms, bathrooms) are present and turn cleanly without jamming.

4. Electrical & Appliance Functionality

Test all light switches, ceiling fans, and socket outlets. If appliances (e.g., air conditioners, stoves) are included, service filters and test run units before the move-in date.

5. Documented Move-In Condition Report

Take dated photographs of every room, wall surface, and meter reading. Have the tenant sign an agreed inventory checklist upon key handover to avoid end-of-lease security deposit conflict.
