Seven Property Maintenance Checks Every Landlord Should Consider

Published by Civitas Editorial • November 2026 • 5 min read

Protecting rental yields starts with preserving building fabric. Landlords who rely strictly on tenants to report faults often discover problems only when water has ruined ceilings or electrical shorts have destroyed appliances.

The Landlord Maintenance Checklist

1. Water Ingress & Ceiling Moisture Checks

Inspect ceilings and exterior plaster for damp patches or peeling paint, especially around roof flashing, parapets, and upper-floor bathroom slab joints. Early silicone re-sealing costs a fraction of ceiling board replacement.

2. Water Pump & Pressure Tank Operation

Booster pumps and poly-tanks are essential in Accra. Check for continuous cycling (a sign of hidden toilet leaks), inspect the pressure switch, and clean inline sediment filters every quarter.

3. Consumer Unit (Breaker Box) & Earthing

Verify that circuit breakers are not overheating, tripping erratically, or showing burn marks. Ensure proper earth rod continuity to protect against surge damage common with grid fluctuations.

4. Air Conditioning Drainage & Condenser Health

Clogged AC drain lines are the leading cause of interior wall water staining. Ensure drainage pipes flow freely away from walls and exterior coils are washed of dirt and salt corrosion.

5. Perimeter Wall & Gate Hardware

Inspect boundary walls for foundation settling, gate hinges for rust, and electric fencing wires for proper tension and vegetation clearance.

6. Plumbing Fixture Seals & Trap Gaskets

Check under bathroom vanities and kitchen sinks for slow, weeping leaks. Slow drips rot MDF cabinetry long before water pools visibly on the floor.

7. Compound Surface Drainage

Verify that compound gutters and soakaways are clear of leaves, sand, and plastic debris ahead of heavy downpours to prevent foundation pooling.
