Civitas Fix Division

Cleaning, Repairs & Maintenance Made Simpler.

Every property needs regular attention. From a thorough cleaning to a minor repair, the right service at the right time can make a meaningful difference.

Civitas Fix brings together practical cleaning and maintenance solutions for residential and selected commercial properties across Accra and Tema.

Section A

Professional Cleaning Services

Thorough, hygienic upkeep carried out by prepared cleaning teams using appropriate solutions and equipment.

Routine Residential Cleaning

Cleaning for everyday living spaces, including agreed floors, surfaces, kitchens, bathrooms, and other accessible areas.

Deep Cleaning

A detailed, intensive cleaning service for spaces requiring extra attention beyond routine upkeep—including baseboards, appliances, and tile sanitization.

Move-In & Move-Out Cleaning

Complete turnover cleaning when preparing a property for incoming tenants or resetting a property after an occupant has vacated.

Upholstery Cleaning

Targeted deep extraction cleaning for eligible fabric and leather upholstered sofas, armchairs, and cushions, subject to fabric suitability.

Pressure Washing

Restoring selected exterior surfaces, paving stones, compound floors, and perimeter walls using appropriate pressure-washing machinery.

Post-Construction Cleaning

Removal of construction-related dust, paint splatter, and general builder residues following renovation, subject to site assessment and clearance.

Section B

Repairs & Practical Maintenance

Fixing faults properly the first time with appropriately competent and vetted technicians.

General Handyman Services

Minor adjustments, shelving installations, bracket fittings, curtain rods, and practical everyday maintenance tasks.

Plumbing Repairs

Selected plumbing faults, leaking pipe joints, toilet siphon repairs, tap replacements, and drainage clearing by competent personnel.

Electrical Repairs

Selected electrical maintenance, socket testing, switch replacements, and fixture repairs undertaken by suitably qualified electricians.

Doors, Locks & Fittings

Re-alignment of sagging or sticking doors, deadbolt servicing, handle repairs, and hardware adjustments.

Painting & Touch-Ups

Targeted patch plastering, wall repainting, skirting touch-ups, and stain cover-ups based on agreed paint codes and scope.

Air Conditioning Servicing

Filter washing, coil sanitization, condenser inspection, and refrigerant verification arranged through qualified technicians.

Minor Tiling Repairs

Selected tile replacement, hollow tile resetting, regrouting of wet areas, and silicone bead renewal, subject to assessment.

How Pricing Works

Every assignment is priced fairly according to its scope, labour requirements, materials needed, transport logistics, and complexity.

Some routine cleaning services have indicative starting prices. Other repair tasks require photographs or a brief site inspection before we can issue an accurate, binding quotation.

Our Scope & Pricing Commitment:

  • ✓ We clearly itemize what is included in the quoted service.
  • ✓ Significant replacement materials and hardware are identified upfront.
  • ✓ No unexpected or hidden add-ons after work commences.
  • ✓ Major repairs and regulated specialist work receive appropriate assessments before quote approval.
