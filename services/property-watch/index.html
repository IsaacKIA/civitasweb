Process

How Property Watch Works

Five clear steps ensuring security, authorized access, and actionable reporting.

01

Tell us about the property

Share its location, occupancy status, property type, and specific inspection priorities via WhatsApp or our enquiry form.

02

Agree on the inspection scope

We confirm legal access authorization, checklist parameters, appointment timing, and inspection fees.

03

The property is inspected

An assigned competent personnel completes the agreed visual checks, verifying electrical, plumbing, walls, and grounds.

04

Receive your report

We provide observations and supporting dated photographs, highlighting issues that may need attention or maintenance.

05

Decide on the next steps

Any identified repairs or follow-up services are separately assessed, quoted, and approved by you before anything is undertaken.

Important Limitations & Legal Boundary:

Property Watch is a periodic inspection and reporting service. It is not continuous security surveillance, insurance, structural engineering certification, or a guarantee against theft, damage, or deterioration.

Answers for Overseas Owners

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about managing your property in Ghana from the UK, US, Canada, or Europe.

How does Civitas Property Watch help Ghanaians living abroad in the UK, US or Canada?

Civitas Property Watch acts as your independent, professional eyes and ears on the ground in Greater Accra and Tema. We carry out scheduled checklist inspections, photograph and verify building elements (roofing, damp, storage tanks, electrical, compound walls), and deliver timestamped digital reports directly to your WhatsApp and email within 24 hours. No awkward family favours or conflicting reports.

How much does a property inspection cost for a diaspora owner in Accra or Tema?

Single comprehensive photographic property inspections start from GH₵ 350. Ongoing monthly oversight subscriptions start from GH₵ 300 per month, which include routine physical checks, water meter/tank monitoring, utility status verification, and priority technician dispatch when repairs are required.

What is included in a Civitas photographic inspection report?

Every report includes high-resolution dated photographs of exterior walls, roof condition, ceilings, plumbing fixtures, overhead water tanks, pumps, electrical distribution boards, and grounds. It flags urgent defects, preventive maintenance recommendations, and verified contractor repair estimates.

Can Civitas coordinate and supervise repairs while I am overseas?

Yes. If an inspection uncovers an issue—such as a leaking pipe or damaged roof sheet—Civitas provides an itemised repair scope and transparent cost quote. Once approved, our vetted technicians complete the repair with full before-and-after photographic sign-off.
