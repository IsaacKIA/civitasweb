Civitas Property Watch Division

Your Property. Better Visibility.

Owning a property does not always mean being physically present to check on it.

Whether you live elsewhere in Ghana or outside the country, reliable information about your property's condition can help you make better maintenance decisions.

Civitas Property Watch is designed to provide scheduled property condition inspections, photographic documentation and practical reporting.

Inspection Services

What We Offer

Structured, objective evaluations of accessible spaces carried out with detailed checklists.

Basic Property Inspections

A scheduled visual assessment of accessible areas within an agreed inspection scope, noting general upkeep and obvious defects.

Detailed Condition Reports

Structured observations supported by high-resolution photographs and practical recommendations for issues requiring attention.

Monthly Property Oversight

Scheduled property checks and updates for customers requiring ongoing visibility throughout the year.

Vacant Property Monitoring

Client Profiles

Who Is Property Watch For?

Solving real oversight challenges for local and remote property stakeholders.

Property Owners Living Abroad

Eliminate reliance on hearsay or burdensome family favors. Receive direct, objective photographic reports of your property in Ghana.

Process

How Property Watch Works

Five clear steps ensuring security, authorized access, and actionable reporting.

01

Tell us about the property

Share its location, occupancy status, property type, and specific inspection priorities via WhatsApp or our enquiry form.

02

Agree on the inspection scope

We confirm legal access authorization, checklist parameters, appointment timing, and inspection fees.

03

The property is inspected

An assigned competent personnel completes the agreed visual checks, verifying electrical, plumbing, walls, and grounds.

04

Receive your report

We provide observations and supporting dated photographs, highlighting issues that may need attention or maintenance.

05

Decide on the next steps

Any identified repairs or follow-up services are separately assessed, quoted, and approved by you before anything is undertaken.

Important Limitations & Legal Boundary:

Property Watch is a periodic inspection and reporting service. It is not continuous security surveillance, insurance, structural engineering certification, or a guarantee against theft, damage, or deterioration.
