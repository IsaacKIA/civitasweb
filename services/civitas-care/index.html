Civitas Care Division

Better Property Care Begins Before Problems Become Expensive.

Regular attention helps property owners understand the condition of their buildings and plan maintenance more effectively.

Civitas Care is our proposed recurring property care service for owners, landlords and property managers who want a more structured approach to inspections and maintenance coordination.

Preventive Logic

Why Choose Planned Property Care?

Property maintenance is easier to manage when responsibilities are clear, inspections are scheduled, and repair needs are identified early.

Scheduled Condition Checks

Routine monthly inspections ensure small leaks, electrical hazards, or roof wear are caught before causing severe damage.

Practical Inspection Updates

Receive concise, readable condition summaries with photographs outlining actionable priorities.

Tracked Maintenance Log

Maintain an organized record of past work, upcoming maintenance requirements, and equipment servicing history.

Coordinated Repair Assignments

When repairs are necessary, approved work is assigned directly to vetted technicians under agreed scopes and timelines.

Proposed Monthly Care Plans

Choose the level of oversight that fits your property type and lifestyle. All plans include dedicated inspection and coordination support.

Essential Care

For property owners who need basic scheduled oversight.

GH₵350 / month
Ideal for: Apartments & small residential units
  • 1 scheduled condition inspection per month
  • Brief written condition summary
  • Preventive maintenance reminders
  • Coordination of separately approved repair work
  • Standard customer support
Recommended

Standard Care

For properties requiring more frequent attention & photo reporting.

GH₵650 / month
Ideal for: Family homes & occupied rental investments
  • Up to 2 scheduled condition inspections per month
  • Detailed photographic reporting & observations
  • Comprehensive maintenance scheduling
  • Priority coordination of approved service requests
  • Dedicated care advisor contact

Premium Care

For properties requiring an involved, active coordination arrangement.

From GH₵1,200 / month
Ideal for: Executive estates & multi-tenant properties
  • Up to 4 scheduled inspections per month
  • Enhanced digital condition reporting
  • Planned preventive maintenance program
  • Priority dispatch for approved service requests
  • Dedicated personal relationship contact

Important Package Information & Exclusions:

Monthly care plans cover the specific inspections, reporting, and coordination services stated in the customer's agreement. Replacement parts, specialist repairs, cleaning assignments, emergency work, and additional unscheduled visits are not automatically included; these are quoted separately unless expressly stated in the signed service agreement.

*Plan suitability, coverage parameters, and final terms are confirmed before enrollment.*
