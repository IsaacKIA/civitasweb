Civitas Care

Planned Property Care & Preventive Maintenance

Property maintenance should not begin only when an expensive failure happens. Civitas Care provides structured monthly inspections, preventive reminders, and organized repair coordination across three tier plans:

  • • Standard Care: GH₵650/mo (2 inspections, photo reporting & scheduling)
