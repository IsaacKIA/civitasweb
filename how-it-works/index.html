Step-by-Step Delivery

The Six-Step Civitas Journey

01

Step 1: Send Your Request

Contact Civitas through WhatsApp or complete the website enquiry form. Tell us your property location, property type, requested service, and preferred schedule.

02

Step 2: We Review Your Requirements

Depending on the service, we may request photographs, additional details, or arrange a site assessment to understand the scope and technical work involved.

03

Step 3: Receive and Approve Your Quote

Your quotation outlines the agreed scope, transparent price, exclusions, estimated schedule, and payment conditions before any commitment is made.

04

Step 4: We Arrange Delivery

We confirm the assigned competent service personnel, required materials and equipment, access arrangements, and appointment details.

05

Step 5: Service and Quality Review

The approved work is performed, and completion is reviewed against agreed requirements. Customers are encouraged to inspect and report any concerns promptly.

06

Step 6: Payment and Follow-Up

Payments are recorded and acknowledged. We follow up for your feedback and can discuss ongoing property care or preventive maintenance where appropriate.
